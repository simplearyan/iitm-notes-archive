Let X denote the number of hours for carpentry.
Let Y denote the number of hours for plumbing.
Let Z denote the number of hours for electrical work.

It is given that

           E(X) = 120, E(Y ) = 60, E(Z) = 12.

Now,

    E[X + Y + Z] = E(X) + E(Y ) + E(Z) = 120 + 60 + 12 = 192

  Therefore, expected number of hours of labor is 192.
