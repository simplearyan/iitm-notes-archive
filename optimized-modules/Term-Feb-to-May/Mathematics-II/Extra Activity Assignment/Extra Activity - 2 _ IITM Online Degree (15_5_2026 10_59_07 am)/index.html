The description of the elements in the project is as follows:

• The parameters a,b,ca, b, c a,b,c and ddd are variables used to determine the linear transformation whose matrix representation with respect to the standard bases is [abcd]\begin{bmatrix}a & b \\c & d \end{bmatrix} [ac​bd​]

• Te1Te1Te1 and Te2Te2Te2 are vectors which represent the images of the standard basis vectors e1e1e1 and e2e2e2, respectively.

• u is an arbitrary vector in the plane, and TuTuTu is its image under TTT.

• SSS is a circular sector in the plane and TSTSTS represents its image under TTT.

To specify the transformation, one can specify the values of a,b,ca, b, c a,b,c and ddd. Once a linear transformation is fixed, the vector u can be moved by dragging its arrowhead. The vector TuTuTu changes accordingly to give the image of the vector uuu.
