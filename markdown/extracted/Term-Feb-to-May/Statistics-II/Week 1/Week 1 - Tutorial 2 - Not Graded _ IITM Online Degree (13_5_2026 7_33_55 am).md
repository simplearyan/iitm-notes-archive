### Feedback:

X denotes the smaller of two numbers.Hence, range of X will be {1, 2, 3, ... , n − 1}.  
Y denotes the larger of two numbers. Hence, range of Y will be {2, 3, 4, ... , n}.  
 Let fXY be the joint probability mass function of X and Y .  
Note that:  
fXY(x, y) = 0 if x ≥ y.  
If x < y:  
fXY (x, y) = P(one ticket is x and another ticket is y)  
⇒ fXY (x, y)  = 2 n(n − 1)
