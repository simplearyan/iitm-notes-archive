# Extra Activity - 1 (Description) :: IITM Online Degree

Extra Activity - 1 (Description)

The first extra activity for has two parts:

*   Activity 1: Create a Google Site as a student portfolio with Statistics 2 as one of the pages inside it. Provide a short and relevant description about the course in this page.
*   Activity 2: Collect data from an interesting source and populate a Google Spreadsheet. The data should have minimum of 100 rows and 5 columns. Describe the rows and columns of this data in your own words and describe something interesting that you learnt/observed from this data.
