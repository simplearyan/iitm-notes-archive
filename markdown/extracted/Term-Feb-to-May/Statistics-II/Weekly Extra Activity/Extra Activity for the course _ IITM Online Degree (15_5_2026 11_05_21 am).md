# Extra Activity for the course :: IITM Online Degree

Extra Activity for the course

###   

As part of the course, we will be assigning weekly extra activities that are tailored to help you exhibit the key learnings from the course. While the extra activities are not mandatory, we strongly urge you to attempt them. These activities will also expose you to practical skills that you will find useful in future (both in the programme as well as your career).

We will also do a peer evaluation of the extra activity that you did so that you will learn the skill of providing critical feedback in this process.

### Grading for extra activities

### 

To incentivize your participation in the extra activities, we have allotted 5 bonus marks for the entire set of activities. The evaluation criteria for this bonus marks is given below:

*   Complete more than 75% activities - 3.75 marks
*   Complete between 50% and 75% of activities - 2.5 marks
*   Complete between 25% and 50% of activities - 1.25 marks
*   Complete less than 25% activities - 0 marks
*   Bonus marks based on the quality of activity - 1.25 marks (Instructors' and Faculty's discretion)

NOTE: Extra Activities may not be present every week. Please check the weekly content carefully to see if there is an activity present.

### Google Sites as Student Portfolio for Extra Activities

All the extra activities done by you have to be updated in your student portfolio. You can create student portfolio using Google Sites or use the one which you have created in Statistics-I course. Refer to the **[lecture video](https://youtu.be/k4oWqYT6tjk?t=1350)** on the steps for creation of Google Sites. Please ensure that you login using your student id and share the site only internally within the IIT Madras Online Degree GSuite Organization

**Note: If a student does not complete required number (at least 5) peer reviews, he/she will be awarded 0 marks for that activity.  
Note : Extra Activity marks will be added only when you pass the course.**
