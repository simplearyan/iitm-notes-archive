$f (x)$ = $\\large(\\frac{2}{3})\\large(\\frac{1}{3})^{x-1} \\text{ for } x = 1, 2, 3, ...$  
  
Find the value of P(max $X\_i$ ≤ 2).

$\\large(\\frac{7}{8})^3$

$\\large(\\frac{8}{9})^3$

$\\large(\\frac{6}{7})^3$

$\\large(\\frac{1}{9})^3$

### No, the answer is incorrect.  
Score: 0

### Feedback:

The only way that the maximum of the Xi will be less than or equal to 2 is if all of the Xi are less than or equal to 2.  
  
That is P(max Xi ≤ 2) = P(X1 ≤ 2, X2 ≤ 2, X3 ≤ 2)  
  
Now, since X1, X2, X3 are i.i.d random variables  
  
P(max Xi ≤ 2) = P(X1 ≤ 2, X2 ≤ 2, X3 ≤ 2) = P(X1 ≤ 2)P(X2 ≤ 2)P(X3 ≤ 2) \[independence\]  
  
⇒ P(max Xi ≤ 2) = \[P(X1 ≤ 2)\]3 \[identically distributed\]
